# Getting started

*From opening Northwind to having your first working report.*

## 1.1 Signing in

Your organisation has its own area of Northwind, called a **[workspace](/docs/glossary#workspace)**.
Its name appears in every address just after `/w/`, and again at the bottom of the left-hand menu,
so you can always check which one you are in.

1. Open the link your administrator sent you. It will look like `https://…/w/your-workspace`.
2. Sign in if you are asked to. Northwind uses your normal work account, so there is no separate
   password to remember.
3. You land on the home screen. You are ready to build.

:::note
If you do not know your workspace name, open `/find-workspace` and Northwind will help you find
it. Following an invitation link joins you to the workspace and brings you straight here.
:::

## 1.2 A tour of the screen

Two things stay on screen wherever you go: the **menu** down the left, and the **bar** across the
top. Learn these two and you will never be lost.

![The Northwind home screen, with the navigation menu on the left and a search bar across the top](/img/undraw_docusaurus_mountain.svg)

*FIG 1.1 — The home screen. One box to type in, and everything else is a way of getting back to it.*

### The menu on the left

The top items are places you visit often. Below them are labelled groups:

| Group | What is in it | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top items | Home, Templates, Reports, Sources | This part |
| Data | Connections, Datasets, Saved questions | [Working with data](/docs/working-with-data) |
| Sharing | Links, Embeds, Scheduled emails | Part 3 |
| Admin | Members, Usage, Settings | Part 4 |

### The bar across the top

The box in the middle jumps you to a page; press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>⌘</kbd> +
<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) from anywhere to open it.

:::danger Read this before you use search
The search box takes you to **pages**, not to your own work. Typing *sales* offers "Sales
dashboards" and "Create a sales report"; typing the name of one of your own reports finds nothing.
To find something you made, open the list it lives in.
:::

## 1.3 Build your first report

1. From the home screen, type what you want in the box — for example,
   *"a dashboard showing revenue by region for the last quarter"*.
2. Press **Send**. Northwind builds it, narrating what it's doing as it goes.
3. When it finishes, you'll have a working page. Ask for changes in the same conversation:
   *"add a filter for product category at the top"*.

:::tip
Ask for one change at a time. Five requests in one message tend to produce four of them.
:::

## 1.4 Start from a template

If you'd rather not start from a blank box, open **Templates** and pick something close to what
you need. A template pre-fills the description, which you then edit before sending.
